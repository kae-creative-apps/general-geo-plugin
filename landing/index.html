The closed loop

Four steps.
Once a month.

Run these in order. You'll always know where you stand — and what to fix next.

01
Audit
Your pages
Score your top and bottom pages against 10 AI-readiness criteria. See which ones need work and why — ranked by priority. Bring a Search Console CSV and the results are weighted by real traffic.
/geo-site-audit
02
Fix
Flagged pages
Give it a URL from the audit. It rewrites or restructures the page so AI engines are more likely to cite it as a source. Fix reports save to Google Drive if you've set that up.
/geo-fix [url]
03
Measure
Share of Model
Runs your 10 anchor questions through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini — live, in your browser. Same questions every month, so the trend actually means something. Takes about 10–15 minutes.
/geo-check  ·  /geo-gaps
04
Create
Content for gaps
Takes any topic AI is answering without citing you. Turns it into a full content brief, optimized for citation from the first sentence. Hand it to any writer — they can run with it immediately.
/geo-brief [topic]
What you get

Everything lands in
Google Sheets.

No new dashboard. No new tool. Results go where your team already works.

01  ·  Monthly
Share of Model score

A percentage showing how often AI recommends your brand across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini — weighted by their actual market share. Tracked month over month so you see movement, not just a number.

02  ·  Per audit
Page-level audit scores

Every audited page scored against 10 AI-readiness criteria with a priority ranking — High, Medium, or Low. You know exactly where to spend time this month.

03  ·  Per gap
Content gap briefs

Topics AI is answering in your space without citing you. Each becomes a ready-to-use brief, built for citation from sentence one. Writer-ready.

All commands

Eight skills.
One plugin.

/geo-setup
First-time onboarding. Configures your brand, connects Google Sheets, and picks your 10 monthly anchor questions. About 5 minutes.
/geo-site-audit
Audits your top and bottom pages for AI readiness. Drop in a Search Console CSV and results are weighted by actual traffic.
/geo-fix [url]
Takes a specific URL and rewrites or restructures it for AI citation. Saves a fix report to Google Drive if you've configured it.
/geo-check
Runs your anchor questions through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini live. Measures Share of Model per engine. Plan on 10–15 minutes.
/geo-gaps
Finds questions AI is answering in your domain where your brand isn't showing up. Your highest-leverage content opportunities.
/geo-brief [topic]
Generates a full content brief for a gap topic, built for AI citation. Ready to hand to any writer, immediately.
/geo-facts
Builds a Fast Facts page to prevent AI from citing wrong information about your pricing, data, or positioning.
/geo-report
Writes a monthly performance summary to your brand sheet. Run this last — after the full loop — to close out the month.
